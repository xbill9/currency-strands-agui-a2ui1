The A2UI 1.0 currency agent, moved to AWS end to end: Strands Agents with Amazon Nova 2 Lite on Bedrock, the agent on AgentCore Runtime over AG-UI, and the Next.js UI on ECS Express Mode.
The model writes one macro call and the server builds the card or chart. Ten prompts, ten runs each: 100 of 100 rendered clean locally and 100 of 100 through the deployed app.
